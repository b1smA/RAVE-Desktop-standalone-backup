# RAVE features

RAVE preserves the replaceable-provider architecture of Nuclear while developing a separate listening experience.

## Source intelligence

- Smart Source ranks multiple candidates by match accuracy, duration, quality, speed, reliability, and preferences.
- Backup candidates allow playback recovery when the selected stream fails.
- Source diagnostics explain selection decisions without exposing sensitive local data.
- Wrong-version feedback can reject live, remixed, altered-speed, truncated, or low-quality candidates.

## Queue and discovery

- Smart Queue avoids unwanted repetition and explains automatic choices.
- Automatic radio continues playback after the manual queue ends.
- Smart shuffle, Replay, Mix, Weekly, forgotten favorites, and release discovery use local listening data.
- Listen Later, pinned playlists, bulk actions, and playlist search improve library workflows.

## Personalization and privacy

- Recommendation snoozing and taste-profile exclusions stay on the device.
- Private moods, tags, notes, artist blocks, family filtering, and sleep controls do not require a cloud account.
- Listening history and local profiles remain under the user's control.

## Desktop experience

- The compact mini player, selectable audio outputs, media controls, loudness analysis, data-saving mode, and resumable downloads are designed for desktop use.
- Artist pages classify and separate singles, EPs, albums, compilations, and live releases while grouping duplicate provider variants.
- RAVE Sessions supports protected local rooms, guest permissions, queue voting, QR invitations, and an activity history.

The in-app **What's New** page is the detailed record of user-visible additions and fixes.
